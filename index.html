About

I am an MPhil student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and a researcher at iHEAR Lab, under the supervision of Prof. Fangxun Zhong. My work focuses on multimodal perception, 3D scene understanding, and vision-language-action systems for embodied intelligence, especially in safety-aware robotic perception and control.

I build research systems that connect perception, reasoning, and action, with experience spanning visual-haptic interaction perception, physics-grounded VLA control, GUI grounding, LLM post-training, and 3D structured spatial reasoning. My primary toolkit includes PyTorch, PyTorch3D, OpenCV, Open3D, PEFT/LoRA, and modern VLM/LLM training pipelines. I am actively seeking internship opportunities in VLA, VLM, multimodal foundation models, embodied AI, and reinforcement learning.

EmbodiedSceneAgent: 3D-Aware Vision-Language Planning for Long-Horizon Manipulation
A flagship embodied AI system that integrates scene memory, planning, verification, and replanning into a closed loop.
Built a closed-loop embodied agent with scene memory, planner, skill executor, verifier, and replanner for long-horizon task execution.
Used simulator teacher-state bootstrapping to stabilize high-level cognition first, then analyzed the gap between teacher-state and predicted memory.
Evaluated the system on CALVIN, RLBench, and VLABench to study long-horizon success, subgoal completion, and recovery behavior.
Representative result: 52% long-horizon task success on a CALVIN representative subset with 3D memory + replanning, +17 pts over a 2D baseline.

Cross-Website GUI Grounding with Verifiable Reward Optimization
A multimodal GUI grounding system for robust cross-website and cross-domain agent perception.
Formulated GUI grounding as an instruction-conditioned multimodal perception problem from screenshot + instruction to target element, box, point, and action.
Combined visual grounding with OCR / DOM candidate generation and reward-based reranking for better cross-website and cross-domain generalization.
Designed a verifiable reward based on element correctness, IoU, click validity, action correctness, and format validity.
Representative result: 71% / 62% / 54% element accuracy on test_task / test_website / test_domain, with reranking bringing up to +8 pts gain.

Verifiable Code Generation Post-Training Pipeline for Open LLMs
An end-to-end LLM post-training pipeline covering SFT, verifiable reward design, RL-style optimization, and execution-based evaluation.
Built an end-to-end post-training pipeline for code generation with data preparation, SFT cold start, GRPO optimization, sandboxed execution, and unified evaluation.
Designed verifiable rewards based on compile success, public test pass rate, format validity, and length constraints.
Compared Base, Structured Base, SFT, and SFT+GRPO under a unified execution-based evaluation protocol.
Representative result: 32% HumanEval Pass@1, 95% compile success, and +11% relative improvement from SFT to SFT+GRPO.

3D Structured Spatial Reasoning for Embodied Perception
A 3D perception bridge that connects object-centric scene representation with relation-aware language grounding.
Built a structured 3D perception bridge from object-centric scene representation to relation-aware language grounding.
Designed a pipeline with object set building, structured language parsing, soft anchor selection, relation scoring, and interpretable diagnostics.
Focused on hard cases such as relation-heavy scenes, same-class clutter, anchor confusion, and paraphrase stability.
Representative result: 61% Acc@1 / 83% Acc@5, with structured parsing bringing +5 pts overall and +8 pts on relation-heavy subsets.
