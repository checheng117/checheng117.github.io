About

I am an MPhil student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Fangxun Zhong at iHEAR Lab.

My current research studies how embodied agents can convert multimodal observations and task instructions into structured, geometrically grounded, and temporally consistent representations for physical interaction. Across my recent work, I investigate three connected questions: what entity an agent should select, where an interaction should be grounded, and when a prediction becomes actionable.

Although some of my current projects use surgical scenes as challenging testbeds for occlusion, contact, uncertainty, and fine-grained spatial reasoning, my long-term goal is domain-general physical intelligence. I am particularly interested in world models and embodied foundation models that learn predictive representations of objects, geometry, motion, and action effects across tasks and robotic embodiments.

I am primarily interested in algorithmic and foundation-model research, including representation learning, action-conditioned dynamics, multimodal pretraining, 3D and temporal grounding, and reliable vision-language-action systems.

I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027 in Physical AI, Embodied AI, World Models, robot learning, and multimodal foundation models.

Selected Research Manuscripts

Selected first-author research manuscripts on grounded perception, interaction, and temporal decision-making for embodied intelligence.

MARS: Structured Semantic-to-Metric Grounding for Embodied Interaction
In Process
First Author · Research Manuscript · 2026
MARS introduces a typed semantic-to-metric interface that separates semantic proposals, dense spatial grounding, and geometric realization for precise embodied interaction. Surgical scenes serve as a high-precision testbed for studying replaceability, intervention, and failure diagnosis in multimodal perception-to-action systems.
Embodied Grounding Multimodal Interfaces 3D Geometry Reliable Physical AI
Absorb3D: Relation-Aware Candidate Matching for 3D Grounding
In Process
First Author · Research Manuscript · 2026
Absorb3D studies the recall-to-conversion gap in 3D grounding, where recovering more plausible object candidates can make correct selection harder. It develops comparative relation-aware matching to reason over competing candidates rather than scoring each proposal independently.
3D Grounding Relational Reasoning Candidate Matching Embodied Perception
PACE: From Scores to Alarms for Budgeted Temporal Anticipation
In Process
First Author · Research Manuscript · 2026
PACE develops a budget-aware evaluation protocol that converts frame-level anticipation scores into matched alarms under lead-time, false-alarm, and cooldown constraints. It studies whether improved prediction actually translates into actionable early warning under deployment budgets.
Temporal Anticipation Reliable Evaluation Decision Protocols Sequential Prediction
