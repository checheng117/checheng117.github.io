About

Hello! I'm Che Cheng, currently pursuing an MPhil in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and conducting research in the iHEAR Lab.

My research focuses on vision-language-action (VLA) systems and learning-driven 3D computer vision for embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in multimodal perception, depth and geometry learning, and safety-aware visual understanding and control for robotic systems.

I have hands-on experience with PyTorch, PyTorch3D, Python, C++, ONNX optimization, large model deployment, and modern AI development workflows.

Research

Multimodal Visual-Haptic Distillation for Interaction Perception
Built a teacher-student interaction perception framework that fuses image segmentation, optical flow, depth estimation, and geometric analysis to extract proxy signals such as proximity and reliability from videos, and distills them into a lightweight real-time model for contact-risk perception in complex scenes.
Multimodal Perception Distillation Interaction Understanding
Page
Physics-Grounded VLA System for High-Risk Embodied Control
Developed a vision-language-action system based on InternVL3.5-8B with dual-head outputs for both physical reasoning and control parameters, integrating SPD control matrix modeling, safety-constrained velocity correction, and a differentiable optimization safety layer for closed-loop embodied control.
